# The frontline plan, Step 1: the three goals measured (2026-11-20)

Step 1 of the [frontline plan](2026-11-20-frontline-plan.md), done on the
owner's go-ahead ("start step 1", then "Refine, then PR", forms of
2026-11-20): the three goals measured in the CodinGame games, before any
change to the bot, by the review tool's new command `codingame-roles`.

## Data and method

- `ice-and-fire-review codingame-roles --data <data branch>/ice-and-fire
  --me Tarleya --players zasmu --release ice-and-fire-v029` (and `--release
  ice-and-fire-v030`, and every release): the games of the data branch
  `claude/codingame-games` (at 528a55f), each side at the start of its ply,
  game turns 2 to 15. The groups: zasmu, the rest of the top 10 of the
  leaderboard snapshot, and our release, by seat; every game is a game
  against us. zasmu played red in only 7 of its games against v029 and we
  played blue in only 69, so the red zasmu row is thin.
- The definitions are the plan's (objectives: the middle chokes and the
  band cells, counted when they decide at least 3 cells of land), with
  variants added when a first definition separated no group (the owner:
  "propose multiple variants ... when relevant"). The tables of the command
  hold all of them; this note keeps those that separate zasmu from us
  within a seat, and says which did not.

## What did not separate

- **A frontline unit as two approaching moves in a row** (the plan's first
  definition): 6 to 16% of the objectives not held, for every group. Also
  the variant "the nearest unit approached in the last ply": 40 to 80%,
  no steady gap.
- **Idle units with no cell to take within 2 steps** (the plan's first
  definition of a stranded back-line unit): 0 to 5% for every group. Our
  units do stand still twice as often as zasmu's (above), but next to
  cells they could take, at the front, not stranded inside our land.
- **Trainings at the tip after a move:** 89 to 96% at game turn 6 for
  every group, then falling alike.
- An objective's value as the land it adds to the side alone (the
  "marginal" value) is 0 for an objective already held, so it cannot count
  objectives held; it was dropped.

## What follows for the plan

- **Goal 1 (frontline units):** zasmu holds about 1.5 times our objectives
  at game turns 8 to 12 counting every choke and band cell, and many times
  ours among those deciding at least 3 cells (as red at game turn 10, 5.1
  against 0.35), and it holds its first one two turns earlier; as red,
  where both sides start alike, it has a unit at our land's tip toward an
  objective in 86% of its turns at game turn 8, we in 44%. So the gap is in
  getting units to the objectives, as the plan says; the definition of a
  frontline unit for the bot is the role it is given (Step 3), not one
  recognised from moves.
- **Goal 2 (the race and local fights):** zasmu's captures are local (over
  90% within 2 steps of an objective, no cut far from them); ours are less
  so (65%, and 10% of our cuts fall far away). A third of all capture plies
  touch two objectives more than 2 steps apart, for every group: fights are
  mostly local, not wholly, so Step 6's regions must check the cuts that
  cross them.
- **Goal 3 (room for the back line):** zasmu keeps 1.5 to 2 times our
  pockets, and its units stand still half as often as ours, while units
  stranded with nothing to take are rare for everyone. So Step 5's target
  is our units that stand still at the front (30 to 40% of unit turns at
  game turns 10 to 12, zasmu 7 to 22%), and the pockets as the cells they
  can take instead.
- **The plan goes on** with Steps 2 and 3 (objectives and their value; the
  race and the roles), as the owner decides.
