# The frontline plan, Step 1: the three goals measured (2026-11-20)

Step 1 of the [frontline plan](2026-11-20-frontline-plan.md), done on the
owner's go-ahead ("start step 1", then "Refine, then PR", forms of
2026-11-20): the three goals measured in the CodinGame games, before any
change to the bot, by the review tool's new command `codingame-roles`.

## Data and method

- `ice-and-fire-review codingame-roles --data <data branch>/ice-and-fire
  --me Tarleya --players zasmu --release ice-and-fire-v029 --top 0`: the
  games of the data branch `claude/codingame-games` (at 528a55f) between
  v029 and zasmu only (head to head: 61 with v029 as red, 7 as blue); each
  side at the start of its ply, game turns 2 to 15.
- The control: the same with `--top 10`, whose "top 10" rows are the
  players ranked 2 to 10 in their games against v029; they tell zasmu's own
  traits from those of the top players in general. (In that run the v029
  rows pool all of v029's games against those players.)
- The definitions are the plan's (objectives: the middle chokes and the
  band cells; "counted" when they decide at least 3 cells of land), with
  variants added when a first definition separated no group (the owner:
  "propose multiple variants ... when relevant"). The command prints all of
  them; this note keeps those that separate, and says which did not. Rows
  of fewer than about 20 sides or cases are left out of the claims; zasmu
  as red and v029 as blue are 7 sides each.

## What did not separate

- **A frontline unit as two approaching moves in a row** (the plan's first
  definition): 6 to 16% of the objectives not held, for every group; and
  "the nearest unit approached in the last ply": from 0.14 to 1.00 with no
  steady gap.
- **A unit on the side's tip toward each objective not held:** the
  head-to-head rows of v029 rest on 1 to 3 objectives after game turn 6
  (it holds few counted objectives to aim from), so they cannot be read;
  in the pooled run the groups overlap.
- **Idle units with no cell to take within 2 steps** (the plan's first
  definition of a stranded back-line unit): 0 to 5% for every group.
- An objective's value as the land it adds to the side alone was dropped:
  it is 0 for an objective already held.

## What follows for the plan

- **Goal 1 (getting units to the objectives):** the gap is large and early.
  As red against zasmu, v029 almost never holds an objective that decides
  land, where the other top players hold 1.5 at game turn 8 and 4 at game
  turn 10; counting every choke and band cell, zasmu and the other top
  players hold about 1.5 to 2 times ours from game turn 8. A frontline unit
  is better defined by the role the bot gives it (Step 3) than recognised
  from moves.
- **Goal 2 (the race and local fights):** the top players' captures are
  local (71 to 94% within 2 steps of an objective; zasmu's most), and
  their cuts fall where they capture; a fifth of our cutting plies cut
  land far from what they captured. In a third of all capture plies the
  cells taken are nearest to two objectives more than 2 steps apart, for
  every group: fights are mostly local, not wholly, so
  Step 6's regions must check the cuts that cross them.
- **Goal 3 (room for the back line):** the players ranked 2 to 10 keep 2
  to 3 times our pockets as red (zasmu as red likewise, over 7 sides).
  Units standing still are a trait of zasmu (as blue, 16 to 22% of unit
  turns at game turns 10 to 12), not of the top players in general (26 to
  33%, as ours: v029 as red 34 to 42%); units stranded with
  nothing to take are rare for everyone. Step 5's target is our units that
  stand still at the front, with pockets as cells they can take instead.
- **The plan goes on** with Steps 2 and 3 (objectives and their value; the
  race and the roles), as the owner decides.
