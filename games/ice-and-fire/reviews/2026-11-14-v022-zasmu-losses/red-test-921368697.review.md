## Lessons

Red (v022):
1. When blue is able to train onto a contact cell (30 gold or more), spend the bank on that line before the enemy turn. At ply 16 the 36 gold could have held (5,5) and (4,5).
2. Put the first towers on the contact line by turn 8, not in the interior. Blue's tower at (7,5) (ply 15) protected the centre; red's first two (ply 18) did not cover anything that was attacked.
3. Do not buy level 3 units late. Each costs 20 upkeep a turn, and at ply 40 they cut income by 38 and material by 106. Keep a reserve and a level 2 guard on the HQ lane instead.

Blue (zasmu), for reference:
1. Early towers on the contact line, and the 30-gold strikes onto exposed enemy cells, were the core of its win.
2. Its east pocket was cut off at ply 41 and stayed cut for several turns. Thin lines are exposed; keep the links to the HQ.
3. Banking gold for one final chain worked here, because the chain reached the HQ. Repeat it only when the path is open.
