## Lessons

**Blue (v022)**
1. Before the next cut, cover a single joint: at ply 15 it had one joint and 36 gold. A tower on a cell next to (6,6), or a level 2 (20 gold), would have made the cut cost red far more.
2. Do not bank gold while the enemy grows. Gold sat unspent at plies 19, 25 and 27 (32, 50 and 74 gold), and the first mine came only at ply 29.
3. Keep expanding after the centre fight. Blue stayed at 27 to 28 cells from ply 18 to 22 while red went from 34 to 46, and red's income ended at 50 against 29.

**Red (zasmu)**
1. Level 2 trainings onto a single enemy link, with a cut, were the cheapest gains of the game (plies 16 and 20).
2. Towers on the front of the centre (plies 14, 24, 32) kept blue's level 1 pushes out.
3. A 15-gold tower at ply 38 left the HQ chain one gold short for two turns. Keep the chain cost in hand when the race is close.
