## Openings

Red's opening (v004) was two level 1 units on (1,0) and (1,1) at ply 0. Blue opened with two level 1 units in its own corner at plies 1, 3 and 5. Red's income led blue from ply 3 to ply 13 (20 against 15 at ply 13), with cells level at 19 each. But the material was level too (185 to 189 at ply 13), and blue had more army (40 to 30). The opening lead did not survive the middle game. The decision was how gold became level 2 units and how the link cells were held. This single game cannot show the effect of v004's weights. Comparing with v003 needs its plies from the batch.

## Lessons

Red:
- Do not hold gold while the link cells are contested. At ply 14 it kept 39 gold and let blue's level 2 take (5,4).
- Reconnect before building. At ply 26 and ply 28 red bought a tower and a unit instead of reconnecting seven and nine cut cells.
- Spend gold on units from turn 15 on. Red waited with 28 to 39 gold at plies 36 and 38 while blue's HQ chain was built.

Blue:
- Do not push a group into a band whose link cells one burst can cut (ply 15).
- Keep the land and army lead it built. Its mines came only at ply 37 (blue had none before), and an earlier mine would have added income in the middle game.
