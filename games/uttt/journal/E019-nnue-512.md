Step D1 of [the roadmap](../ROADMAP.md#d-scale), width 512. The owner chose the fit on 2026-10-14 ("Both (Recommended)") and this plan the same day ("Ok with your plan"). #130 made the room: with the network, the bot's bundle took 97,457 characters.

**The fit** ([report](../training/runs/38634035143/report.md)) used the same 2 million games of v008's search and the same split as the 256 network of E017, for 12 epochs instead of 8, in 58 minutes. On the same 4,182,825 held-out positions:

| Network | Epochs | Squared error | Cross-entropy, nats |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| (189 → 256) × 2 → 10, run 38562652097, quantized | 8 | 0.1498 | 0.6199 |
| (189 → 512) × 2 → 10, run 38634035143, at its 8th epoch | 8 | 0.1503 | 0.6209 |
| (189 → 512) × 2 → 10, run 38634035143, quantized | 12 | 0.1493 | 0.6186 |

The gain is small, and comes from the extra epochs rather than the width: after 8 epochs the wider network was behind. On 80 million positions of one bot's games, the data, not the size, seems to limit the network.

**Screening** against `uttt-v012` in the arena (4-ply openings, seed 1, 3 games at a time on Claude's 4-core session):

| Time per move | Tolerance | Pairs | Wins, draws, losses | Elo against v012 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 20 ms | 15 ms | 100 | 49, 86, 65 | -27.9 [-66.4, +10.0] |
| CodinGame's limits | 0 | 50 | 20, 59, 21 | -3.5 [-41.6, +34.5] |

Each evaluation costs about twice as much, so the search judges fewer positions per turn, and the slightly better estimates do not make up for it: no gain at full time, a loss at 20 ms. No release and no SPRT. The bot change was only the weights file and the width in `main.rs`; the weights stay on the run's branch `claude/train/38634035143` until the Prune branches workflow deletes it (ADR 0021).

What this says for step D: a wider network needs more and better data first (step D2: more games, then games of the NNUE bot itself), and a faster evaluation (D3), before width pays.
